# THI LinkedIn – CCOI / HL7 FHIR Connectathon

## Main Post Draft

**Breaking down barriers in healthcare data exchange.** The CCOI Imaging Standards and Interoperability Work Group is launching a new HL7 FHIR Connectathon, and we're proud to be part of this critical initiative.

This Connectathon brings together healthcare technology leaders to advance interoperability standards that enable seamless data exchange across systems and devices. By working together, we're addressing one of healthcare's most persistent challenges: ensuring that patient data flows efficiently and securely between different platforms, ultimately improving care delivery and outcomes.

THI is committed to supporting these efforts, recognizing that true innovation in healthcare technology requires collaboration and standardized approaches. This Connectathon represents an important step forward in making healthcare data more accessible, actionable, and meaningful for clinicians and patients alike.

Join us in following this important work. Together, we can build a more connected healthcare ecosystem.
