What draws me to Anthropic is having watched AI transform an industry from the inside—and seeing how much work it takes to get it right.

At Optos, I spent months curating retinal imaging datasets for deep learning models. The technical work was fascinating, but what stuck with me was watching researchers and clinicians try to figure out how to actually use these models. The gap between what the AI could do and what partners needed to understand to deploy it—that's where everything fell apart or came together. When I moved to Topcon Healthcare, I saw the same pattern from the marketing side. We were launching innovation programs like Healthcare from the Eye™ and working with KOLs, startups, and health systems. The technology was there, but the messaging, the enablement, the way we helped partners understand what was possible—that's what determined whether something scaled or stayed a pilot project.

That's why partner marketing at Anthropic feels like the right next step. I've seen what happens when AI companies build great technology but don't invest in helping their ecosystem understand it, position it, and sell it. I've also seen what happens when they do—the 200% growth in engagement I drove at Topcon came from aligning marketing with operational priorities across EMEA and the Americas, making sure five different teams could actually use what we built. That cross-functional, cross-regional work taught me that scalable programs aren't just about resources—they're about building systems that work whether you're talking to a startup or a health system, whether you're in London or San Diego.

My dissertation at St. Andrews was on how AI transforms clinical decision-making and professional boundaries in eye care. What I found was that the companies that succeed aren't just the ones with the best models—they're the ones that help their partners navigate the transformation. That's what partner marketing at Anthropic is really about. When an ISV integrates Claude, they're not just adding a feature. There's also something about Anthropic's approach to safety that resonates with what I've learned working in healthcare. In my experience, the companies that win long-term are the ones that build trust, not just features. When I'm talking to a partner about co-marketing, I need to believe in what we're selling. Building marketing programs that can scale across that spectrum while still feeling personal and valuable to each partner—that's the kind of problem I want to solve. I want to work at Anthropic because this is where the real work is happening. Not the hype, not the demos—the actual infrastructure for how businesses will use AI. And I want to be the person who helps partners understand that, communicate that, and build businesses on it.
