## 1.2 Social Media Foundation

### Instagram Strategy (Target: 5K → 15K followers in 6 months)

**Content Pillars:**
1. **Behind-the-Scenes (30%)** — Studio sessions, vinyl digging, pre-show rituals
2. **Performance Content (25%)** — High-quality clips from sets, crowd reactions
3. **Lifestyle/Aesthetic (20%)** — Travel, food, Italian influence, fashion
4. **Educational/Engaging (15%)** — "Track ID" posts, production tips, gear tours
5. **Community (10%)** — Reposts, shoutouts, fan engagement

**Posting Schedule:**
- Feed posts: 4-5x per week
- Stories: Daily (minimum 3-5 per day on event days)
- Reels: 3-4x per week (critical for growth)

**Key Tactics:**
- Collaborate with visual artists for custom graphics
- Create recurring series: "Pistacchio's Pick of the Week" (track recommendations)
- Location tag every post for discoverability
- Engage authentically with 20-30 accounts daily in the scene

### TikTok Strategy (Target: 0 → 25K followers in 6 months)

**Content Types That Work for DJs:**
1. **"POV" content** — "POV: You're at my set when I drop [track]"
2. **Transitions** — Outfit transitions, location transitions, before/after show
3. **DJ tricks & tutorials** — Quick mixing tips, beatmatching, effects chains
4. **Reaction content** — Reacting to crowd moments, to classics, to new releases
5. **Day-in-the-life** — "What a touring DJ actually does in a day"
6. **Trending sounds** — Adapt trends to DJ/music context
7. **Italian angle** — "Italian things that just hit different" + music overlay

**Posting Schedule:**
- Minimum 1x daily (2x preferred during growth phase)
- Post between 6-10pm local time for peak engagement
- Use trending sounds within first 48 hours of trend emergence

**Viral Hooks to Test:**
- "I'm an Italian DJ and here's what American clubs don't understand..."
- "This is the track that changed my life"
- "Watch the exact moment 500 people lost their minds"
- "What €50 vs €5000 DJ equipment sounds like"

### YouTube/YouTube Shorts
- Launch channel with DJ sets (filmed properly — single camera minimum, multi-cam preferred)
- Post one 1-hour mix monthly
- Cross-post TikTok/Reels content to Shorts

## 1.5 Booking Strategy (Phase 1)

### Current Market Analysis
Before aggressive booking pushes, establish:
- Professional EPK (Electronic Press Kit)
- High-quality DJ photo set (minimum 10 images)
- 3-5 recorded sets (video preferred)
- Clear genre positioning
- Professional rate card

### Target Venue Types (Phase 1 — Proving Grounds)
| Venue Type | Goal | Examples |
|------------|------|----------|
| Underground clubs | Build credibility | Basement venues, warehouse parties |
| Bar residencies | Consistent visibility | Cocktail bars, hotel lounges |
| Day parties | Build following | Rooftop events, summer series |
| Support slots | Association with bigger names | Opening for touring artists |

### Booking Approach
1. **Research thoroughly** before reaching out — know the venue's vibe, past bookings
2. **Personalize every inquiry** — reference specific events they've done
3. **Offer value** — guaranteed crowd, promotional support, unique programming ideas
4. **Start local** — dominate your home market before expanding
5. **Accept strategic free/low-pay gigs** — visibility over income in early phase

### Create a Residency
Pitch a **monthly residency concept**:
- "Gusto" — A monthly night celebrating Italian-influenced house music
- Handle promotion, curation, branding
- Builds consistent local following and creates content opportunities

# CLOSING NOTES

The path from emerging to established artist is not linear. Expect:
- Slow periods and sudden jumps
- Rejections and breakthroughs
- Algorithm changes and platform shifts

**Core principles to maintain throughout:**
1. **Consistency beats virality** — Show up every day
2. **Authenticity is irreplaceable** — Stay true to the sound and story
3. **Relationships are everything** — Invest in genuine connections
4. **Quality over quantity** — Better to release less but better
5. **Play the long game** — Career, not moment

DJ Pistacchio has all the ingredients for cult status. With disciplined execution of this plan, the next 12 months will establish the foundation for a lasting career.
