RECRUITER PERSPECTIVE - CLOSING SENTENCE:

I want to work at Anthropic because this is where the real change is happening—and I want to be the person who helps partners understand that the companies that win in the long run are the ones that build on technology designed to last. That's the conversation I want to have, and that's the foundation I want to help build.

---

WHY THIS WORKS FROM A RECRUITER'S PERSPECTIVE:

This closing works because it:
1. Shows deep understanding of Anthropic's mission (not just surface-level interest)
2. Demonstrates they "get it" - they understand the philosophy vs. capability distinction
3. Shows personal commitment ("I want to be the person who...")
4. Ties to long-term thinking (companies that win in the long run)
5. Ends with action ("the foundation I want to help build")
6. Feels authentic - not generic AI company talk

As a recruiter, I'd think:
- "This person understands what we're actually trying to do"
- "They're not just looking for a job - they believe in the mission"
- "They've thought deeply about the partner challenge"
- "They have relevant experience (healthcare AI, trust-building)"
- "They're early career but show the right mindset"
- "This is someone I'd want to take a chance on"

The closing sentence should feel like a natural conclusion that makes the recruiter think: "I need to talk to this person."
