## 1. MASTER PROJECT OVERVIEW

### Project Purpose, Scope, and Objectives

**THINC EMEA** is the establishment of a new innovation center and clinical demonstration facility in the EMEA region, aligned with Topcon Healthcare's strategic vision for innovation and clinical enablement.

**Scope:**
- Design and fit-out of a multi-purpose facility combining clinical, educational, demonstration, and office spaces
- Integration of advanced IT/AV infrastructure to support clinical demonstrations, training, and remote collaboration
- Procurement and installation of clinical equipment for demonstration and training purposes
- Furniture and space planning to create a functional, professional environment

**Objectives:**
- Create a world-class innovation center that showcases Topcon Healthcare technology
- Enable clinical demonstrations and training for customers, partners, and internal teams
- Support remote collaboration and virtual demonstrations through advanced AV/IT systems
- Establish a professional environment that reflects Topcon Healthcare's brand and values
- Align with THINC SD (San Diego) innovation center strategy and approach

### Alignment with THINC SD / Innovation Center Strategy

THINC EMEA is designed to mirror and complement the THINC SD innovation center model, creating a consistent global approach to:
- Customer engagement and demonstration capabilities
- Clinical training and education programs
- Technology showcase and innovation development
- Remote collaboration and virtual engagement

This alignment ensures that both centers can work together to support global customer needs and maintain consistent standards for clinical demonstrations and training.

### High-Level Timeline and Milestones

**Phase 1: Planning & Design (Completed)**
- Initial project planning and scope definition
- Space planning and layout design
- Vendor identification and initial discussions

**Phase 2: Construction & Fit-Out (In Progress)**
- Construction/fit-out work
- Infrastructure installation (electrical, data, etc.)
- Fire safety and building compliance

**Phase 3: Furniture & Furnishings (In Progress)**
- Furniture selection and procurement
- Installation and placement
- Final adjustments

**Phase 4: IT/AV Infrastructure (In Progress)**
- AV system design and installation
- IT infrastructure setup
- Digital signage and presentation systems
- Video conferencing capabilities

**Phase 5: Clinical Equipment (In Progress)**
- Clinical equipment procurement
- Installation and calibration
- Integration with IT/AV systems

**Phase 6: Clinical Enablement (Pending)**
- Final system integration and testing
- Staff training on systems and equipment
- Operational readiness

**Current Status:** Project is in active execution phase, with multiple workstreams progressing in parallel. Construction/fit-out is underway, furniture decisions are being finalized, IT/AV systems are being specified, and clinical equipment procurement is in progress.
