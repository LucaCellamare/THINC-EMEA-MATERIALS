### Purpose, Scope & Objectives

THINC EMEA is the European counterpart to THINC SD (San Diego), serving as Topcon Healthcare's innovation and demonstration center for the EMEA region. The facility will function as a:

1. **Clinical Research & Demonstration Hub** - Modular exam suites with diagnostic stations for showcasing Topcon ophthalmic equipment and clinical research-ready space. Clinical research will be conducted in this space. Patients participating in clinical trials will be here.

2. **Training & Education Center** - Flexible conference/training spaces with AV capabilities for customer training and stakeholder engagement e.g. clinicians, healthcare systems, partners, collaborators, internal/external team.

3. **Innovation Showcase** - Display area for full range of Topcon diagnostic equipment including OCT, fundus cameras, slit lamps, and refraction systems

4. **Office Space** - Workspace for Topcon EMEA staff (located on the first floor) and startup incubator areas (located on the second floor)

5. **Strategic Meeting and Networking Site** - for KOLs, health systems, industry partners, stakeholders

### Alignment with THINC SD / Innovation Center Strategy

- Mirrors THINC SD model for customer experience and clinical demonstration
- Provides EMEA-based venue for equipment demonstrations, clinical trials, workspace, and training
- Enables startup partnerships and innovation collaborations in EMEA market
