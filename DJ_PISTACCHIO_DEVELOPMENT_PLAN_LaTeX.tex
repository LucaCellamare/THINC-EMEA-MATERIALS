\section{Executive Summary}

\begin{executivebox}
DJ Pistacchio represents the next wave of European-influenced house music breaking into global consciousness. With a distinctive sonic identity blending house/tech-house grooves, eclectic Italo-disco, and sultry, groove-heavy sets that evoke the seductive sophistication of Hotel Costes, Luca has the foundational elements for cult status. His sound is warm, sexy, and unmistakably European---the kind of music that transforms a room.

\vspace{0.5em}

This plan outlines the strategic roadmap to transform him from an emerging talent to a recognized name in the underground-to-mainstream pipeline.
\end{executivebox}

\vspace{1em}

\noindent\textbf{Strategic Objectives:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Establish distinctive brand identity and visual language
    \item Build substantial social media presence (50K+ combined following)
    \item Secure label releases and industry recognition
    \item Develop touring infrastructure and booking relationships
    \item Cultivate authentic community and cult following
    \item Initiate strategic brand partnerships
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Appendix A: EPK Contents Checklist}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item[$\square$] High-resolution press photos (minimum 5)
    \item[$\square$] Artist bio (long: 300 words, short: 100 words, one-liner)
    \item[$\square$] Music links (Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube)
    \item[$\square$] 3--5 best DJ set recordings with tracklists
    \item[$\square$] Press quotes/reviews (when available)
    \item[$\square$] Upcoming shows
    \item[$\square$] Social media links and follower counts
    \item[$\square$] Technical rider
    \item[$\square$] Contact information
\end{itemize}

\section{Closing Remarks}

The path from emerging to established artist is not linear. Expect:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item Slow periods and sudden acceleration
    \item Rejections and breakthrough moments
    \item Algorithm changes and platform shifts
\end{itemize}

\vspace{1em}

\noindent\textbf{Core Principles to Maintain:}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Consistency beats virality} --- Show up every day
    \item \textbf{Authenticity is irreplaceable} --- Stay true to the sound and story
    \item \textbf{Relationships are everything} --- Invest in genuine connections
    \item \textbf{Quality over quantity} --- Better to release less but better
    \item \textbf{Play the long game} --- Career, not moment
\end{enumerate}
